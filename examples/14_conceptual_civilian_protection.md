# 14. How civilian protection changes in hostilities

## Question

> How does civilian protection change when civilians directly participate in hostilities or when civilian sites are used for military purposes?

## Reference answer

Civilians lose protection from direct attack if they engage in combat or otherwise take a direct part in hostilities. Likewise, a civilian or protected object can lose its protected status if the adversary uses it for military or combat purposes, making it subject to attack, though proportionality still matters when civilians or protected objects are nearby.

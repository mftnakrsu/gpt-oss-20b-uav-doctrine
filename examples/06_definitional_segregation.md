## Question

> In detainee operations, what does segregation mean, and for what kinds of reasons can it be used?

## Reference answer

Segregation means removing a detainee from other detainees and from that environment for legitimate reasons not related to interrogation. It may be used when required for movement, health, safety, or security involving the detainee, the detention facility, or facility personnel.

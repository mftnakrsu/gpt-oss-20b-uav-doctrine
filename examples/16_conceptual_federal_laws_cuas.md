## Question

> Why have federal criminal laws traditionally limited law enforcement options for countering drones?

## Reference answer

Many counter-UAS techniques can trigger existing federal legal restrictions. Intercepting signals may raise issues under surveillance laws such as the Wiretap Act and Pen/Trap Statute, while jamming, spoofing, or hacking can implicate laws protecting communications systems, satellite operations, and computer access; physically disabling a drone may also run into statutes that protect aircraft from sabotage or piracy.

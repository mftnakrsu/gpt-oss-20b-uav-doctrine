# 12. What commanders must assess before activating an AWS

**Source:** `icrc_2025_position_autonomous_weapons_ihl/ICRC-Position_Paper-Autonomous_Weapon_Systems_and_IHL-Selected_issues_Oct2025.pdf`
**Question type:** procedural
**Judge winner:** A (v4)

## Question

> What must commanders or other AWS users assess before activating the system?

## Reference answer

They must assess in advance the legality of all possible strikes the system could carry out. They have to ensure every foreseeable strike would comply with IHL, regardless of what triggers it, across the full area and entire period the AWS remains active.

## v4 (fine-tuned) — 49 tokens

They must assess the expected effects of the system’s operation in the specific circumstances and conditions of use. That assessment has to cover the full area where the AWS may be used and all possible situations that could reasonably be expected to occur during its operation.

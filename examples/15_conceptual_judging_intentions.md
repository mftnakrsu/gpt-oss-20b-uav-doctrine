## Question

> Why does the doctrine say it is especially hard to judge an adversary’s intentions in modern operations?

## Reference answer

The text explains that identifying intentions has always been difficult, and it is even harder in contemporary and future environments. One reason is that unconventional or hybrid tactics can make raw military size less useful as an indicator, so intelligence staff must help commanders appreciate the greater difficulty in judging both capabilities and intentions.

## v4 (fine-tuned) — 42 tokens

It is especially difficult because modern adversaries can move quickly, hide effectively, and use deception. These factors make it hard to determine what they actually want and what they are capable of doing.

## Question

> Why does doctrine say support areas need protection planning on par with close areas?

## Reference answer

If a support area is degraded, it can seriously undermine overall operations and reduce the force’s ability to shape the deep area for brigade combat teams conducting close operations. Because of that operational impact, planners should give support area protection the same level of attention as protection in close areas.

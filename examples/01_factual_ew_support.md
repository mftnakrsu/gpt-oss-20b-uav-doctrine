## Question

> What is electronic warfare support used for, and how can its data support operations?

## Reference answer

Electronic warfare support is used to search for, intercept, identify, and locate sources of radiated electromagnetic energy so commanders can recognize threats immediately and support targeting, planning, and future operations. It helps reduce uncertainty in the battlespace and can feed signals intelligence, support electronic or physical attack targeting, and produce measurement and signature intelligence.

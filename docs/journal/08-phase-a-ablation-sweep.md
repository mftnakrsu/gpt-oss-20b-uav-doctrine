## Ablation design

Single-axis variants vs the v1 baseline so each delta is attributable. Per the 2026 Unsloth GPT-OSS guidance, the reasoning-renderer variant was deliberately omitted — switching to `gpt_oss_medium_reasoning` on Q&A pairs **without** CoT traces in the assistant turn doesn't add reasoning capability and can suppress it. A reasoning-render experiment requires regenerating training data with CoT first (Phase A.5).

| `v2` | rank 16 → **32** (+capacity) |

| `v4` | lr 1e-4 → **2e-4** (+step size) |

**Reference:**

> Coordination is needed when multiple force elements operate in the same engagement space and may involve procedural control based on how much they interact and how much shared situational awareness they have. Dynamic synchronization allows even greater interaction and mutual support in changing situations, but it depends heavily on good communications, shared situational awareness, and responsive commanders.

**`v1`:**

**`v2`:**

> Coordination is the exchange of information and decisions to resolve conflicts and make sure actions are mutually supporting. Synchronization is the deliberate arrangement of military actions in time, space, and purpose so they produce the desired effects. Dynamic synchronization is a more flexible, responsive form of coordination used when conditions change rapidly or are uncertain, especially in complex or asymmetric operations.

**`v3`:**

**`v4`:**
